Study at your own pace.

Paceory combines a study timer, subject and book records, learning goals, and daily, weekly and monthly statistics. Use your recorded time per question or page to plan a realistic workload.

Core planning, timing and study records are free. Optional ads and a monthly auto-renewing ad-free subscription are available in supported regions. The price and terms displayed by Google Play apply. Study records stay on your device; export a backup before deleting the app or changing devices.
